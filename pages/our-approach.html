Our Approach

A working method, not a magic formula.

We follow a simple, transparent process that keeps you informed and in control — from first conversation to steady-state improvement.

  1. Home
  2. Our Approach

The four phases

How an engagement typically unfolds.

1

Understand Your Needs

We listen to the business outcome you want and the problems standing in the way. This phase shapes everything that follows.

2

Review Your Data & Systems

We map your current data landscape — sources, flows, quality issues and dependencies — with facts, not assumptions.

3

Design the Right Solution

You receive a clear design with priorities, effort and technology choices, explained in plain English.

4

Implement & Improve

We build with your team, test carefully, launch cleanly, and keep improving based on how it behaves in real use.

What you can expect at each phase

No surprises, ever.

Discovery

A focused conversation: goals, current state, and an honest read on what is realistic.

Assessment

A structured review of your data and systems, delivered as a readable, decision-focused report.

Design

Solution architecture and a prioritized roadmap, validated with you before any building starts.

Build & Support

Incremental delivery, testing and monitoring — followed by knowledge transfer to your team.

Working principles

  • Deliver in small, reviewable stages.
  • Document as we go — no knowledge hoarded in heads.
  • Use technology you can actually maintain.
  • Keep security and quality baked in, not bolted on.

Location & logistics

SHANZOO is based in Toronto, Ontario. We deliver remotely across Canada and internationally, with on-site sessions in the Toronto area when useful.
