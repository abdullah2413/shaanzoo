Why Data Quality Matters

Bad data is not an IT problem — it is a business problem.

4 min read Educational
  1. Home
  2. Insights
  3. Why Data Quality Matters

Every decision your business makes runs on data: forecasts, inventory, pricing, hiring. When that data is quietly wrong, problems surface as lost revenue and frustrated customers — never as 'a data-quality incident.'

What is data quality?

Data quality is how well your data serves its purpose. It is commonly described with six dimensions: accuracy (is it correct?), completeness (is anything missing?), consistency (does it agree across systems?), uniqueness (any duplicates?), timeliness (is it current?) and validity (does it obey the rules?).

The cost of bad data

Bad data shows up in uninviting ways: duplicate mailings and wasted ad spend; quotes built on stale prices; inventory that does not match reality; and leadership teams arguing over which dashboard is right. Fixing the underlying data is usually far cheaper than living with the consequences.

Dimensions of quality in practice

Improvement starts with profiling — measuring how bad things actually are against those six dimensions. Then you fix the specific problems found, standardize how data enters systems, and add monitoring so quality does not quietly decay again.

Getting started

Begin with the datasets your biggest decisions depend on, not all your data at once. Measure today's state, fix the worst offenders, and assign an owner for each key dataset. A small, sustained program beats a large one-off cleanup every time.

How SHANZOO helps

We run a data-quality health check, show you the exact issues and their business impact, then help you fix root causes and set up the monitoring and ownership that keep quality high.
