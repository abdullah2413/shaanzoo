What is data management?

It is the discipline of keeping your data in good shape.

Think of data management as housekeeping for your business information. It covers the people, policies and technology that keep data accurate, secure and understood so the rest of the business can rely on it.

Without it, the same customer can have five records, reports disagree, and nobody can say for sure who is allowed to see sensitive data.

  • One consistent view of customers, products and suppliers.
  • Clear ownership and shared definitions for key data.
  • Data that is monitored and cleaned continuously.
  • Sensitive information protected by design.

A simple way to think about it

Start with the data that matters most, then build outward.

Define

Agree on what 'customer', 'product' and 'supplier' mean, and who owns them.

Govern

Set rules, responsibilities and approvals for how data is created and changed.

Cleanse

Fix duplicates, gaps and errors — then monitor so they stay fixed.

Protect

Control access, mask sensitive values and keep an audit trail.

Model

Design storage and models that are organized, scalable and future-ready.
