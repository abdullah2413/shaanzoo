The business problems it solves

Sound familiar? This is what we fix.

Nobody owns the data

No one is accountable for whether key datasets are correct, complete or current, so problems go unfixed.

Words don't mean the same to everyone

Finance, sales and IT each define 'active customer' or 'revenue' differently, causing constant disputes.

Audit and privacy requests are painful

You cannot quickly show where data came from, who changed it, or who is allowed to use it.

Key benefits

What you gain.

Clear ownership and accountability

Every important dataset has a named owner and agreed responsibilities.

One business language

Shared definitions mean fewer arguments and more accurate decisions.

Faster, less stressful audits

Lineage and catalog information let you answer 'where did this data come from?' in minutes.

Defensible privacy practices

Governance helps you demonstrate responsible handling of personal data.

Technical capabilities

The detail, for those who like it.

  • Governance operating model and RACI decisions
  • Business glossary and data dictionary
  • Data cataloguing and classification
  • End-to-end data lineage
  • Policies, standards and stewardship workflows
  • Data-quality rules and KPI reporting
  • Connections to collaborating tooling (cloud, BI, MDM)

Common business use cases

Where this service is most valuable.

Regulated financial reporting

Foundation for control frameworks such as GDPR, BCBS 239 and Basel III — where demonstrating oversight of risk data is expected.

Cloud migration governance

Keep track of what data moves to the cloud, who can access it and how it is catalogued.

Self-service analytics enablement

Let business users find and trust approved datasets through a well-maintained catalog.
